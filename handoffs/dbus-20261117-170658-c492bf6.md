## Summary

`dbus` (continue), data bits 0-3. Added 48 transistors (the block now has 96, 0 errors, 0
warnings; 40 of its 40 equations firm).

- **Traced**: under each of the buffer chains 0-3 there is a transparent latch (cell
  `dbus_lat`, ten transistors: two cross-coupled AND-OR-invert gates and an inverter) and a
  static inverter with a strong load that drives the data bus bit (cell `dbus_drv`, two
  transistors). Per bit # = 0..3:
  `d#_d_n = NOT d#_d`; `dl#_q = NOT((d#_d_n AND dbus_le) OR dl#_q_n)`;
  `dl#_q_n = NOT((d#_d AND dbus_le) OR dl#_q)`; `db_b# = NOT dl#_q_n`.
  So `db_b#` = pin D# latched while `dbus_le` is high, and **the internal data bus is driven
  all the time by a static inverter: there is no tri-state driver at this end**.
- **Traced**: `dbl`'s four data lines each end on the output of one of these inverters (a
  buried contact on its node): with `dbl` loaded, `db_b0`..`db_b3` are joined to the D0-D3
  latches. That answers `dbl`'s open question (gate or node).
- **Traced**: the latch copies under chains 1-3 are upside down; the bus inverter repeats at
  the chain pitch, not flipped. The first notes' "cluster 0 + 1366" was wrong.
- **Traced**: the latch enable `dbus_le` is the vertical metal x 17243-17262 from (17252,8990)
  to y 13614, west to the pad (17160,13612), poly west to the pad (16990,13612), metal west
