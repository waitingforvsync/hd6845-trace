- The data pad output cell (traced), placed on D0-D7: `d#_pu = NOR(d_hiz, d#_out_n)`,
  `d#_pd = NOR(d_hiz, d#_pu)` (two depletion loads, four pre-driver pull-downs), 3 pull-up
  and 5 pull-down output fingers. `d_hiz` (formerly P_x, common) high floats the pin;
  `d#_out_n` (formerly P_y, per bit) low drives the pin high. AMB-003 settled.

  frame) is tied to VSS by an outer strip and a rail contact.

  per copy (traced; D7's drawn to x 17920).

- Pins `d0`..`d6` are the protection nodes; `d0_in`, `d1_in` (dbus) and `d2_in_u`..`d6_in_u`
  are aliases of them.
