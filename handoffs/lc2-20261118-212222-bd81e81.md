## Re-read items

Proposed trap for `notes/tracing-guide.md` (it misled me for one commit here, on the branch only):

- **A row that lines up with a poly beyond a rail's edge is not thereby the same poly.** In this cluster the gate rows
  end at the rail's east edge and a different poly (the gate of a pull-down east of the rail) starts 20-30 px further
  on, in line with the row. At 2.8x the two look like one row. Check for the field stripe between them at 5x or more
  in two shots, and ask what each pad on a shared thin metal is on: the reading came apart when the "same row's" other
  pad turned out to be on another gate's load poly.
- Also: **dark edge pixels read near zero in red-minus-green in the chip-2 stripped shot**, like field. A "field gap"
  of under about 10 px measured that way between two dark lines is not evidence of field (nor of diffusion).

Blocks possibly affected: any gate column read with rows running out from under a rail (not known to have happened
elsewhere; `lc3`'s and `lc1`'s gate columns are the same build as this cluster). I do not propose a Re-read item unless
the coordinator knows of a block traced at low zoom with that pattern.
