## Re-read items

Two lessons from this run, for the traps of `notes/tracing-guide.md` (no earlier block known to be affected):

- **A load's plate is not always beside its output's diffusion: follow the plate's poly to its tie first.** Here the
  load of one gate sits under another net's landing pad, 100 px from its own output; its gate comes round by a long
  poly over field, and its source by a 14 px diffusion strip between two polys. I first read the plate as a second
  load on the pad's net and the long poly as a long weak load (W/L 14/175). A W/L like that is itself the warning.
- **In the clean shot a "ring" in the open gap between two metals can be an artefact** (the ring at
  (4832-4850, 8920-8942), absent from both chip-2 shots and both metal shots: review round 13): check a ring in a
  second chip before calling it a buried contact.
